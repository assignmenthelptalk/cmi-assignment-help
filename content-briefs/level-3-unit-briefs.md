- **Performance factors:** Skill, knowledge, attitude, motivation, physical environment, personal circumstances. Use in AC1 to identify the range of factors affecting individual performance.

- **AC1:** Identify the communication methods used within an organisation
- **AC2:** Describe barriers to effective communication and how to overcome them

### Key Theories and Frameworks
- **Shannon-Weaver Communication Model:** Sender, encoder, channel, decoder, receiver, noise/feedback. Use for AC1 to describe how communication works and where breakdown occurs.

- **Barriers to communication:** Physical (noise, distance), semantic (jargon, language), psychological (stress, emotion), organisational (hierarchy). Use for AC2 — describe each category and explain strategies to overcome them.
- **Audience adaptation:** Adjusting language, tone, and channel for different audiences (team members, senior managers, external stakeholders). Core to AC3.

- **Coaching vs mentoring distinction:** Coaching is typically short-term, performance-focused, directive or non-directive, often with a skills goal. - **Benefits of coaching and mentoring in management:** Individual development, retention, performance improvement, psychological safety. Use in AC2 to explain the principles underpinning both approaches.
